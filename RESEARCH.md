# Superhero — KHUD RESEARCH + GYAAN YAAD (v3.5)

## Gyaan Yaad — gyaani aadmi wala tareeka
Poora internet yaad rakhna mumkin nahi — isliye Superhero jaise gyaani
aadmi karta hai, waisa karta hai:
1. **Padho** (sirf padhna — internet par kuch bhejna kabhi nahi),
2. **Khaas baatein apne shabdon me** apni **gyaan-yaad** me note karo
   (`memory/knowledge.jsonl` — vishay, notes, kahan se padha, tareekh),
3. **Dobara kaam aane par pehle wahi yaad dekho** — mile to wahin se
   jawab, na mile to tab padho.
Gyaan-yaad app me **"GYAAN YAAD"** hisse me dikhti hai (kya padha, kab
padha) aur seekhne ki diary me bhi darj hoti hai.

## Khud Research
- Hy bole: **"research: <vishay>"** (ya "X par research karo") — Hy ka
  ye aadesh hi padhne ki ijaazat hai.
- Superhero pehle khojta hai, 1–2 pate padhta hai, samajhta hai, phir
  **apne shabdon me saaf natija/notes** banata hai — copy-paste nahi.
- Notes gyaan-yaad ke saath ek **rachna-file** (`memory/creations/`)
  bankar bhi rehte hain — notes/plan/khaaka yahi hai.

## Khud Skill Banana
- Research se koi behtar tareeka mile to Superhero use **apni skill**
  ki shakl me likh kar rakh leta hai — Skills list me **🛠️ "khud ki
  banayi"** nishaan ke saath dikhegi, diary me darj hogi.
- Koi seekha kaam **baar-baar (3+) aaye** to uske seekhe kadmo se bhi
  woh khud skill bana leta hai.
- **Hy chahe to kabhi bhi hata sakta hai:** bas bole —
  **"skill hatao: <naam>"**.

## Seema — sabse upar wale taale
- **Creator Suraksha Lock aur permission lock in sab se upar hain** —
  research ya khud ki banayi skill unse paar kabhi nahi hogi.
- Bina ijaazat **na file khulti hai, na internet par kuch bheja jata
  hai** — internet ka kaam sirf *padhna* hai, woh bhi Hy ke aadesh par.
- Khud ke banaye task/skill Hy ko app me **hamesha dikhte** hain.
- Khud ka research-task (self-task) internet wala hissa **🔒 ijaazat**
  bankar khada rehta hai — bina Hy ke woh research khud nahi chalegi.
